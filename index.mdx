DTS is an open-source tool that automates translation of GameMaker dialogue strings — built specifically for Undertale and Deltarune fan translations. It extracts strings from `data.win` via UndertaleModTool, sends them to an AI provider of your choice in parallel batches, post-processes the output, and re-injects the translated strings back into the game file. Both a full CLI and a graphical interface are included.

## How DTS Works

DTS orchestrates a five-step pipeline from raw game binary to translated strings:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Extract strings from data.win">
    UndertaleModTool (UMT) reads the game's binary and produces a `strings.json` file containing every string in the game. DTS can trigger this automatically or read an existing file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate translation input">
    `dts input-generate` parses `strings.json` and extracts only the dialogue strings (identified by GameMaker's internal key pattern), writing `lang_input.json`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Translate with your AI provider">
    `dts run` sends dialogue batches to your configured provider (Gemini, Ollama, OpenAI, etc.), accumulates results into `lang_es_out.json`, and auto-retries failed chunks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Normalize and clean">
    `dts normalice` cleans typographic quotes, optionally strips accents for ASCII-only targets, and removes empty entries. `dts voids` removes zero-length translations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply and export">
    `dts apply` injects the translated strings back into the original `strings.json` structure, producing `strings_es.json` ready for UMT reimport — or export directly to a patched `data.win`.
  </Step>
</Steps>
